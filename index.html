Project 1: M3ssag1n8 and OwlDB

By far one of the coolest, yet most tedious school projects I have worked on. M3ssag1n8 was a Slack style messaging application built in TypeScript, but what made the project especially interesting was connecting it with OwlDB, a NoSQL database my team and I built from scratch with Go. I worked across both sides of the project, from building messaging features and user interactions to working with HTTP requests, CRUD operations, JSON validation, and testing. There were definitely moments where debugging drove me crazy, but seeing two projects we built ourselves actually work together made this one of the most rewarding projects I have worked on at Rice.

Project 2: Clean Water Sweep

Clean Water Sweep is a JavaScript game inspired by Minesweeper and built around charity: water's mission to bring clean water to communities around the world. What started as a simple idea changed a lot as I kept building it. Instead of adding more and more features, I focused on making the game feel complete. I added difficulty modes, polished the design, improved the gameplay, and made it feel more connected to charity: water's mission and brand. One thing this project reinforced for me is that sometimes the biggest improvement is not adding more, it's simplifying what you already have and making it better. Every update pushed me to think more about user experience, cleaner JavaScript, and building something people would actually enjoy playing.
